📘 Daily Tracker Guide

1. Two-Way Sync & Smart Auto-Fill

When you select a date (or click ◀ / ▶), the app automatically checks if you already logged anything for that day and pre-fills it so you never lose progress.

2. History & Trends Tab

Switch to the History & Trends tab to view a 7-day movement chart, inspect past days, and edit previous entries with 1 click.

3. Google Sheets Live Link

Click Sheets ↗ in the header to open your live Google Sheet in your web browser immediately.

4. Custom Quick-Add Chips

Click + Add Custom Item to add your own favorite meals or snacks to the quick-add chip list.

5. Ready for Android APK

This entire frontend is mobile-first and configured for 1-command compilation into an Android APK with Capacitor.
